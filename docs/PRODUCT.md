# SAVIOUR

> Never let the door close on you.

## Vision

SAVIOUR is an AI-powered career assistant that helps students discover, organize, and act on opportunities without missing important deadlines.

## Problem

Students receive hundreds of emails related to internships, placements, hackathons, scholarships, and interviews. Important opportunities often get lost among promotions and unrelated messages.

## Solution

SAVIOUR connects securely with Gmail, identifies career-related emails, extracts important information, and presents everything through an intelligent dashboard.

## Target Users

- College students
- Internship seekers
- Fresh graduates
- Placement aspirants

## Core Features

- Smart Inbox
- Deadline Tracking
- Opportunity Hub
- AI Career Assistant
- Smart Reminders
- Privacy-first Gmail Integration

## Product Philosophy

Users stay in control.

AI helps users make better decisions but never replaces them.
